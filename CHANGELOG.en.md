# Changelog

Newest first. Mac builds live right here, in the

`mac-v*` tags; iPhone versions ship through the App Store.

[Русский](CHANGELOG.md)

## Mac

### 1.0.2 — 27 August 2026 ([image](https://github.com/alvlsk12345/scribla-releases/releases/tag/mac-v1.0.2))

The dictionary no longer stretches the window. A long list folds into a single
line with a counter; opened, it scrolls inside itself, and a search field appears
once there are eight words or more.

### 1.0.1 — 25 August 2026 ([image](https://github.com/alvlsk12345/scribla-releases/releases/tag/mac-v1.0.1))

An interim build; no separate notes were written for it.

### 1.0 — 17 August 2026 ([image](https://github.com/alvlsk12345/scribla-releases/releases/tag/mac-v1.0))

The first Mac version: an icon in the menu bar, dictation on the right ⌘ in any
window, an answer on ⌘ + ⌥ and a translation with **/** held down. The same four
languages and the same replacement dictionary as on the phone.

## iPhone

### 1.2.1 — 26 August 2026

The microphone comes back on its own. A call, an alarm or Siri would take the
microphone, and it stayed closed until you opened Scribla by hand — sometimes you
found out halfway through a sentence. Now it wakes a second after the system lets
go of it.

Also in this version:

- The word bar above the keyboard no longer disappears when you touch your own
  text with a finger.
- Calc mode understands percentages in multiplication and division: “120,000
  times 80 percent”, “2400 divided by 15 percent”. And numbers like “five
  thousand four hundred and ten”, even when they come out half in digits.
- Polish gives up less often on a slow connection and stops showing service
  messages when the server is busy.
- In English, Spanish and Chinese, polish runs on the phone itself — instant and
  offline. Apple Intelligence has to be on.

### 1.2 — 20 August 2026

The first App Store release: a keyboard with four modes — text, calc, translate
and AI — word-by-word editing, the replacement dictionary and four languages
recognised on the device.
